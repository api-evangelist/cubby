# Cubby

AI-native facility management software for self-storage operators.

Replaces legacy FMS systems with better UX, embedded payments, and a growing integration ecosystem.

Cubby is built for SMB and mid-market self-storage operators in the US.

Primary products:
- Facility management
- Tenant communications
- Embedded payment processing
- Integration platform

## Product

### Self-Storage FMS
Full facility management — units, tenants, leases, automations.

### Tenant Communications / Comms AI
AI-powered tenant messaging and follow-up.

### Payment Processing
Embedded payments with no per-transaction markup model.

### Integrations
Native connections to access control, insurance, marketing, and more.
